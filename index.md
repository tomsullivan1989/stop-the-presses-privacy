# Stop the Presses - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Stop the Presses is a single-player word game for Android. This page sets out
exactly what data the app uses, and why.

## Summary

The game itself runs entirely on your phone and collects nothing about you.
The only data that leaves your phone is what Google needs to **show ads
between books** and, if you choose to buy it, to process the one-off
**Remove ads** purchase. You can remove ads entirely, and you can decline
personalised ads.

## What is stored on your phone

The app saves your game on the phone, in its private storage:

- The book you are currently writing, so you can carry on later
- Your progress: unlocked typefaces, muses and proof marks, your shelf of
  finished books and your records (best word, best book and so on)
- Your settings, such as whether sound is on, and whether you have bought
  Remove ads

This data is visible only to Stop the Presses, is never sent to us, and is
deleted when you uninstall the app. You can also wipe it at any time from
**How to play, then Reset progress**.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stop the Presses shows a full-screen ad between books, never during one, using
**Google AdMob**. To choose and show ads, Google may collect:

- Your device's advertising ID
- Device and app information (model, operating system, app version)
- Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
- How you interact with ads

Where the law requires it (including the UK and the EU), the app asks for your
consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can
choose personalised or non-personalised ads, and change your choice later.
You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings
(**Settings, then Privacy, then Ads**).

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Purchases (Google Play)

**Remove ads** is a one-off purchase made through **Google Play**. Google
handles the payment; we never see your card or account details. The app only
learns whether the purchase has been made, so it can switch ads off and
restore the purchase on a new phone.

## Sharing a book

When you tap **Share**, the app draws your book's cover as an image on your
phone and hands it to Android's share sheet. Nothing is uploaded unless you
choose an app to send it with, and then that app's own policy applies.

## What is not collected

- **No account or sign-in.** The app never asks for your name or email.
- **No analytics or tracking** of how you play.
- **No contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.**
- **No selling of data.** We do not receive or sell any personal data.

## Children

Stop the Presses is a general audience game and is not directed at children
under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a revised
